The loneliness isn't being alone. It's being in a room full of people and not one of them seeing you.

Immigrants feel it in concentrate. You're suspended between two worlds, fully at home in neither, and a part of you belongs to both. It makes you lonely. It also makes you open. Entrohub is for people who have the fire and just aren't wired together yet.

Connection needs three things in the room.

It doesn't happen on its own. When all three are present, it does. It doesn't have to be deep. Real can happen in the middle of fun.

Trust

We're the same kind of people. Nobody here has to perform.

Permission

You're allowed to walk up to a stranger. Be direct. Be real.

Collision

Different backgrounds, different roads, put in one room. That's where things come out.

What we are not.

  • Not networking.
  • Not a place to collect cards, hunt for resources, or sell yourself.
  • Not a performance for other people to watch.

How an evening works.

Enough structure to make connection easy. Never so much it feels like a workshop.

01

You apply

Tell us who you are and what you're building. We keep the room balanced on purpose.

02

You show up

A relaxed venue, around twenty people, two light rounds that break the ice for you. No pitching. No stage.

03

You leave with people

Not business cards. Conversations worth continuing next week.
