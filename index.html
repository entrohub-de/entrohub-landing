Most events give you contact. Very few give you a next step.

Many of us moved to Germany to build something. We know a lot of people and still don't know who to ask for the one thing we need right now. Entrohub keeps the room small and the question specific, so the right people actually find each other.

How an evening works.

Enough structure to get to the point. Never so much it feels like a workshop.

01 · ~40 min

Same backgroundEngineers with engineers, the rest together

Start with people who speak your language. Say the one thing you came for tonight.

02 · ~40 min

Different backgroundShort turns, then switch

Put your question in front of people who see it from the other side. Write down who can help.

03 · open

Free roundsGo back to the names you wrote

Follow up while you are still in the room. Leave with one person and one next step.

What you take home.

  • One name worth messaging next week, and why.
  • One concrete next step on the thing you came with.
  • A room you can come back to.

Not a pitch night, and not a place to collect cards.
